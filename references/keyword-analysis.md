# Keyword Analysis Framework

## Keyword Research Process

### 1. Seed Keywords
Identify core terms from:
- Services offered (hematologista, transplante de medula, hemograma)
- Location (cidade, bairro, estado)
- Patient intent (agendar consulta, sintomas, tratamento)
- Competitor analysis (what competitors rank for)

### 2. Keyword Categories

| Category | Intent | Example | Priority |
|----------|--------|---------|----------|
| Transactional | Ready to act | "agendar hematologista NH" | Highest |
| Commercial | Comparing options | "melhor hematologista Porto Alegre" | High |
| Informational | Learning | "o que faz um hematologista" | Medium |
| Navigational | Finding specific | "Dr. Nome consultório" | Low |

### 3. Long-tail Strategy
Combine modifiers with seed keywords:
- [service] + [location]: "hematologista novo hamburgo"
- [condition] + [treatment]: "leucemia tratamento quimioterapia"
- [question] + [topic]: "quando procurar hematologista"
- [service] + [qualifier]: "transplante medula óssea especialista RS"

### 4. Keyword Mapping
Assign 1 primary + 2-3 secondary keywords per page:

| Page | Primary Keyword | Secondary Keywords |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| Home | hematologista [cidade] | transplante medula, terapia celular |
| Sobre | dr nome hematologista | formação, HCPA, currículo |
| Áreas | hematologia clínica | anemia, leucemia, linfoma |
| Contato | agendar consulta hematologista | telefone, endereço, convênios |
| Blog Post | [topic-specific] | [related terms] |

### 5. Content Gap Analysis
Compare your pages against top 10 SERP results:
- Topics they cover that you don't
- Questions they answer
- Depth of content (word count, sections)
- Media types (video, infographics, tables)

## Keyword Density Guidelines
- Primary keyword: 1-2% (once per 50-100 words)
- Secondary keywords: 0.5-1%
- LSI/related terms: natural usage throughout
- Never keyword-stuff; prioritize readability

## Tools for Keyword Research
- Google Search Console (existing rankings)
- Google Autocomplete (suggestions)
- "People Also Ask" boxes
- Google Trends (seasonality)
- AnswerThePublic (question-based)
- Ubersuggest (volume estimates)

## Measuring Keyword Performance
Track monthly:
- Ranking position (Search Console)
- Impressions and clicks
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Pages per keyword
- Conversion from organic traffic
